About MapScribe AI

MapScribe AI represents the cutting edge of historical cartography digitization, combining state-of-the-art artificial intelligence with deep understanding of historical map characteristics.

Our system uses advanced deep learning models trained specifically for historical map text detection and recognition. Whether you're a historian, researcher, librarian, or cartography enthusiast, MapScribe AI helps unlock the textual information hidden in historical maps.

  • Spatial coordinate mapping
